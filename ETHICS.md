# Research Ethics And Publication Policy

## Collection Rules

- Use passive, publicly accessible sources only.
- Do not register for criminal forums or private channels for this project.
- Do not contact sellers, threat actors, victims, or targeted employees.
- Do not purchase access, credentials, malware, exploits, or data.
- Do not download leaked datasets, credential dumps, malware, or exploit code.
- Do not probe, authenticate to, or exploit candidate infrastructure.
- Stop collection when a source requires unsafe interaction or personal-account access.

## Data Minimisation

- Do not reproduce credentials, victim records, private phone numbers, or personal data.
- Report the existence and scope of exposed data without storing the data itself.
- Use only the minimum screenshot excerpt required to support an analytical statement.
- Remove workstation metadata and personal-account information before publication.

## Intelligence Hygiene

- Defang domains, URLs, IP addresses, and actor-controlled infrastructure.
- Distinguish observed facts, third-party reporting, actor claims, and analyst inference.
- Record source and collection dates.
- Assign confidence based on evidence quality and source independence.
- Treat actor claims and leak-site posts as allegations until independently corroborated.
- Label candidate and historical indicators to prevent inappropriate blocking.

## Publication Rules

- Public reports use `TLP:CLEAR` or `Public Portfolio Case Study`.
- Original assessment prompts and scoring rubrics are not redistributed.
- Third-party screenshots are cropped, attributed, and used only as supporting excerpts.
- Findings about identifiable organisations are phrased proportionately and include limitations.
- Published material must not create a new privacy, safety, or security risk.
